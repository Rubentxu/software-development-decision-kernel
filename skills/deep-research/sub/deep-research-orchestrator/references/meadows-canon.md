# Meadows Canon — Bibliografía primaria

Fuentes primarias verificadas para Donella Meadows y Systems Thinking. Toda cita atribuida a Meadows, Forrester, Senge, Kim debe poder trazarse a uno de estos documentos.

## Donella Meadows — Obras principales

### *Thinking in Systems: A Primer* (2008)
- **Editorial**: Chelsea Green Publishing, White River Junction, Vermont.
- **Editado por**: Diana Wright (Sustainability Institute).
- **ISBN**: 978-1-60358-055-7.
- **Estructura**:
  - Introduction: The systems lens
  - 1. The basics (elementos, interconexiones, propósito)
  - 2. A brief visit to the systems zoo
  - 3. Why systems work so well
  - 4. Why systems surprise us (delays, nonlinearities, bounded rationality)
  - 5. **System traps... and opportunities**
  - 6. **Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System** (los 12 puntos)
  - 7. **Living in a World of Systems** ("Dancing with Systems")
  - Appendix: System Definitions, Summary of System Principles, Springing the System Traps, Places to Intervene, Guidelines, Model Equations, Bibliography
- **Fuentes verificadas**:
  - PDF: `https://research.fit.edu/media/site-specific/researchfitedu/coast-climate-adaptation-library/climate-communications/psychology-amp-behavior/Meadows-2008.-Thinking-in-Systems.pdf`
  - PDF: `https://naturocracy.org/meadows.pdf`
  - Excerpt (cap. 6): `https://www.gc.soton.ac.uk/files/2015/01/Thinking-in-Systems-145-165.pdf`

### "Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System" (1997)
- **Publicación original**: *Whole Earth Review*, Winter 1997.
- **Expanded paper**: Sustainability Institute, 1999.
- **Fuente canónica archivada**: `https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/`
- **Inspiración**: reunión NAFTA a principios de los 90, donde Meadows vio que se proponía un sistema grande con mecanismos de gestión inefectivos.

### *The Limits to Growth* (1972)
- **Autores**: Donella H. Meadows, Dennis L. Meadows, Jørgen Randers, William W. Behrens III.
- **Editorial**: Universe Books, New York.
- **ISBN**: 0876631650.
- **Patrocinador**: Club of Rome.
- **Acompañado de**: modelo World3 (DYNAMO → STELLA → Vensim → World3-2004).
- **Fuentes verificadas**:
  - PDF: `https://www.donellameadows.org/wp-content/userfiles/Limits-to-Growth-digital-scan-version.pdf`
  - HTML: `https://collections.dartmouth.edu/archive/text/meadows/diplomatic/meadows_ltg-diplomatic.html`
  - eBook: `https://collections.dartmouth.edu/ebooks/meadows-limits-1972.html`
  - Club of Rome: `https://www.clubofrome.org/publication/the-limits-to-growth/`
  - World3 (Wikipedia): `https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World3`

### "Dancing with Systems" (2001)
- **Publicación original**: *Whole Earth Review*, Winter 2001.
- **Fuente canónica**: `https://donellameadows.org/dancing-with-systems/`
- **Adaptado** en *Thinking in Systems* cap. 7.
- **Cita clave**: "We can't control systems or figure them out. But we can dance with them!"

## Jay W. Forrester — Obras fundacionales

### *Industrial Dynamics* (1961)
- **Editorial**: MIT Press, Cambridge MA.
- **Libro fundacional** del campo System Dynamics.
- **Contexto histórico**: el problema del empleo oscilante en plantas de General Electric en Kentucky.

### *Urban Dynamics* (1969)
- MIT Press. Aplicación a sistemas urbanos.

### *World Dynamics* (1971)
- **Editorial**: Productivity Press, Portland OR.
- **ISBN**: 0-262-56018-6.
- **Predecessor** de World3 / Limits to Growth.

### "Some Basic Concepts in System Dynamics" (paper seminal)
- Sloan School of Management, MIT.
- PDF: `https://classes.cc.gatech.edu/AY2018/cs4803cc_spring/research_papers/Forrester-SystemDynamics.pdf`
- Cita clave: "All systems, everywhere, consist of these two kinds of concepts — stocks and flows — and none other."

## Peter Senge — System Archetypes

### *The Fifth Discipline* (1990)
- **Editorial**: Doubleday, New York.
- Cap. 5: positive/negative feedback loops.
- Cap. 6: **Systems Archetypes** (Limits to Growth, Shifting the Burden).
- Cap. 7: principle of leverage.
- Cap. 8: caso People Express.
- Appendix 2: 8 arquetipos restantes.
- Resumen: `https://leeds-faculty.colorado.edu/larsenk/learnorg/senge.html`

## Daniel Kim & Virginia Anderson — *Systems Archetypes Basics*

- **Editorial**: Pegasus Communications, Workbook WB002E.
- **PDF**: `https://thesystemsthinker.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/Systems-Archetypes-Basics-WB002E.pdf`

## Sustainability Institute / Academy for Systems Change

- **Fundada por Meadows en 1996** como Sustainability Institute (Hartland, Vermont).
- **Hoy**: Academy for Systems Change (`https://www.academyforchange.org/`).
- **Programas**: Climate Interactive (En-ROADS, C-ROADS), Sustainable Food Lab.

## MIT OCW 15.988 — System Dynamics Self Study

- **URL**: `https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/15-988-system-dynamics-self-study-fall-1998-spring-1999/`
- **Profesor**: Nelson Repenning (MIT Sloan).
- Tutoriales: "Building a System Dynamics Model", "The First Step", Vensim PLE tutorial.

## John Sterman — *Business Dynamics* (2000)

- McGraw-Hill. Texto estándar de System Dynamics en MBA.
- Cap. 5-7: causal-loop diagrams y stock-flow.
- Cap. 14: policy analysis.
